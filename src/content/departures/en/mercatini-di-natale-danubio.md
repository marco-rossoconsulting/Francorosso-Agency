There are two ways to see the Christmas markets of central Europe. One involves three cities, two hotel changes and a good many hours on a coach. The other is this: the ship moves at night, you wake up moored in a different capital, and the market is a few minutes' walk from the gangway.

The M/S Vivienne is a 140-passenger river vessel, refurbished in 2020, with its restaurant and public rooms facing the water. The visits in Vienna, Budapest and Bratislava are included in the price and run in Italian, with the cruise management and tour staff on board throughout.

It works well for anyone who wants Christmas without the effort: one suitcase, short distances, and the freedom to decide each day whether to follow the group or go your own way.
