## What we collect

We collect the data you give us voluntarily through the forms on this site: your name, email address, telephone number and the details of the journey you describe. No sensitive data is requested and we ask you not to enter any in the free-text fields.

The site uses no profiling cookies and no advertising trackers. The map on the Contact page is provided by Google Maps and loads only when you open it.

## Why we process it

We use your data solely to:

- answer your request and prepare a quotation;
- manage the contract if you decide to book;
- meet legal obligations, in particular accounting and tax obligations.

The legal basis is your consent, the performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps, and compliance with legal obligations.

## Who it may be shared with

Organising a journey requires passing certain data to the suppliers involved: airlines, hotels, operators, insurers and border authorities where required. We pass on only what that specific service needs. Some of these suppliers are based abroad, including in countries that do not offer protection equivalent to Switzerland's; in that case we apply the safeguards required by law.

## How long we keep it

Requests that do not lead to a booking are kept for a limited period. Data relating to journeys actually booked is kept for the period required by Swiss accounting and tax rules.

## Your rights

You may at any time ask to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its processing, by writing to francorosso@francorosso.ch. You also have the right to contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

## Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection. Messages sent through the forms are collected by the hosting service and forwarded to the agency's mailboxes.
